As a visual designer, I would like to differentiate the expandable area content from the rest.
I would like to make sure that users don't mix things up.
It can be as simple as making the text slightly greyer.

The concern is that the reasoning panel content (Input, Sources & Tools, Reasoning sections)
could be confused with the main AI answer content below it. Visual treatment should make the
panel feel clearly secondary/supporting — metadata about the answer, not the answer itself.
