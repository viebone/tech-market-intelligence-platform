> ok can you create a spec on tmip to make sure that every story that we add to the front-end
> has a visual appearance like the last one that we added? the goal is to engage people and
> make it a bit fun, it has to use charts, and combination of visual elements to make more
> impact with the story

The last story shipped is "What we know about the market" (`market-data-briefing`), rebuilt
2026-09-06 to be chart-first (`changes/2026-09-06-market-story-visual-and-dedup.md`): a
framing line, then a stack of `RankedBarList` blocks and a pay-transparency figure + meter,
de-duplicated from the "About this platform" welcome.

The concern: right now the visual quality and shape of a story is whatever the person who
builds it decides. There is no standard that guarantees the next story looks and feels like
that one — chart-first, a mix of visual elements, engaging, a bit of fun, high-impact. The
stakeholder wants that standard written down so every future catalogue entry meets it.

Related: `research/2026-09-06-market-story-visual-and-dedup.md`,
`research/2026-09-04-welcome-visual-data-points.md`.
