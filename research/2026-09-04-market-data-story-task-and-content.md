The market-data story should be a separate task tab in the left navigation, placed directly
above "Tech market hiring status". Selecting it should open the answer as its own task rather
than presenting it as an inline suggested question beneath the hiring-status experience.

The question "What do we currently know about the tech job market?" should produce a concise,
generic briefing. It should use friendly, business-oriented language for end users, avoid
technical terms, avoid overwhelming detail, and focus on what the data means at a high level.
Technical provenance and data detail may remain available through the existing transparency
surface, but should not dominate the visible answer.

The project also needs a reusable content skill that explains how to write end-user content:
plain language, friendly tone, business orientation, concise structure, and no implementation
terminology in the visible product copy.
