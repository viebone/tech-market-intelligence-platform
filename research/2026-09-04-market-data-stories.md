The product should clearly separate and document a growing catalogue of predefined data
stories. A data story is a fixed question and answer structure whose numbers are computed
from the platform's current owned data at request time. The first story should answer:

"What do we currently know about the tech job market?"

It should explain when data collection began, how many jobs have been collected, how many
companies and sources contributed, the main job titles and role categories, the main skills,
and any other useful facts for a professional trying to understand the market. The format
should be prepared in advance for a fast answer, while the data remains current. The design
should leave room for more sources and more stories later, including data from company
websites, layoff portals, papers, and other ingestion runs.
