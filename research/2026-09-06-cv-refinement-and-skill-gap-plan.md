Research indicates users would value a capability that helps them **refine their CV against
the market and get a plan to close their skill gaps**.

The shape users described:

1. **Tell the user which skills are needed** — for the roles / market they care about.
2. **Ask the user for their experience** — collect the extra detail that does not fit on a
   CV (context, depth, projects, situations) by prompting them for it, rather than parsing a
   document alone.
3. **Return an evaluation** of the user's skills against what the market is asking for.
4. **Return a plan to cover the gaps** — concrete steps to get from where they are to what
   the market wants.

This is a signal only — no outcome, priority, or design decision attached yet. Note it points
past the current market-reading scope (`outcomes/understand-market-health-before-searching.md`)
toward acting on that read: a personalised, per-user assessment and gap plan. It also implies
new capabilities not in the platform today — user-supplied profile input, a skills-vs-market
comparison, and generated guidance.
